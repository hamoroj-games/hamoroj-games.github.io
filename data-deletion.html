Data Deletion – Tap Toybox

App: Tap Toybox (com.mohamad.minigames) · Developer: Hamoroj · Last updated: October 3, 2026

Tap Toybox has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, or phone number. This page explains how to delete the data related to your use of the App.

Step 1 – Delete your game data on your device

Your progress (level, coins, scores, settings) is stored only on your device.

  1. Open your phone's Settings › Apps › Tap Toybox › Storage and tap Clear data, or
  2. Uninstall the App. This deletes all game data on the device and stops all further data collection.

Step 2 – Reset your Advertising ID

In your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads), choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. This disconnects your device from previously collected advertising and attribution data.

Step 3 – Request deletion by email

Send an email to fishandsharkgame@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Tap Toybox". We will reply within 30 days and delete any data we hold that can be linked to you.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: game progress on your device (Step 1); analytics data that can be linked to your device, on request (Step 3).
  • Automatically deleted: usage data in Firebase Analytics (games played, scores, time spent, levels, device and country information) is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Kept by third parties: data processed by Google AdMob and Tenjin is retained according to their own policies (Google, Tenjin).
