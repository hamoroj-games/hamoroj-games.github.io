Data Deletion – Tap Toybox

App: Tap Toybox (com.mohamad.minigames) · Developer: Hamoroj · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Tap Toybox has no email or password accounts and never asks for your email or phone number. For the leaderboards and for playing with friends, the App uses an anonymous player ID together with the nickname, avatar, and scores you see in the Profile screen, and stores your friend code, friends list, and head-to-head results. This page explains how to delete the data related to your use of the App.

Step 1 – Delete your leaderboard profile and friends data

Uninstalling the App does not remove your online data by itself. To delete it:

  1. Open the App and tap your avatar at the top of the home screen to open your Profile.
  2. Note your nickname and the player ID shown at the bottom of the screen (it starts with "ID:").
  3. Send them by email to fishandsharkgame@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Tap Toybox".

We will delete your profile (nickname, avatar, XP, best scores, and weekly leaderboard entries) and your friends data (friend code, friends list, friend requests, head-to-head results, online status, invitations, and match data) within 30 days and confirm by email. Your entry will also be removed from your friends' lists. If you no longer have the App, send your nickname and we will do our best to find your profile.

Step 2 – Delete your game data on your device

Your progress (level, coins, scores, settings) is stored on your device.

You can also remove any friend yourself at any time with the × button in the Friends screen.

  1. Open your phone's Settings › Apps › Tap Toybox › Storage and tap Clear data, or
  2. Uninstall the App. This deletes all game data on the device and stops all further data collection.

Step 3 – Reset your Advertising ID

In your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads), choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. This disconnects your device from previously collected advertising and attribution data.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: your leaderboard profile and friends data (Step 1) and analytics data that can be linked to your device.
  • Deleted regularly: old match invitations and match data.
  • Deleted by you: game progress on your device (Step 2).
  • Automatically deleted: usage data in Firebase Analytics (games played, scores, time spent, levels, device and country information) is deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Kept by third parties: data processed by Google AdMob and Tenjin is retained according to their own policies (Google, Tenjin).
