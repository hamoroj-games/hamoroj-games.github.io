Privacy Policy – Tap Toybox

Developer: Hamoroj · App: Tap Toybox (com.mohamad.minigames) · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when playing Tap Toybox ("the App"), how it is used, and available choices. The App is developed by Hamoroj ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • The App has no email or password accounts and never asks for your email, phone number, or photos.
  • Your game progress (level, coins, scores, settings) is saved on your device.
  • For the leaderboards, the App creates an anonymous player ID and stores your nickname, avatar, XP, and best scores online. Other players can see your nickname, avatar, and scores.
  • If you use the Friends feature, your friend code, friends list, online status, and game invites are stored online so you can play with friends.
  • The App offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Third-party services from Google (AdMob and Firebase) and Tenjin (attribution partner) are used to show ads, run the leaderboards, understand App usage, and measure advertising.
  • The App is not directed to children under 13.

2. Leaderboards and player profile

To show global and weekly leaderboards, the App stores the following on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to an anonymous player ID created by Firebase Authentication:

  • Nickname: a random name (for example "Player4821") that you can change in the Profile screen. Please do not use your real name.
  • Avatar: the character and color you pick in the App.
  • Game results: total XP, XP earned this week, and your best score in each mini-game.
  • Anonymous player ID and the time of the last update.

Your nickname, avatar, and scores are visible to other players in the leaderboards. Your player ID is shown at the bottom of the Profile screen so you can use it for data requests.

3. Friends and online play

The Friends feature lets you add friends and play live matches with them. It works only between players who added each other; there is no matchmaking with strangers. Linked to your anonymous player ID, the App stores:

  • Friend code: a random 6-character code that others can use to send you a friend request.
  • Friend requests and friends list: who you sent requests to, who accepted, and when (on Cloud Firestore).
  • Online status: whether you are currently in the App, in a game, or in a match, so your friends can see if you are online (on Firebase Realtime Database).
  • Invites and matches: game invites between friends and the data needed to run a live match (moves, scores, connection delay). Match data is used only during the match.

Your friends can see your nickname, avatar, level, and online status. You can remove a friend at any time in the Friends screen.

4. In-app purchases

The App offers optional purchases (for example gem packs, removing ads, and a starter pack). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under Google Payments' privacy notice. We do not receive or store your name, card number, or other payment details. The App receives only the purchase record from Google Play (product, order status, and a purchase token) to deliver your items and to restore purchases such as "remove ads" on a new install.

5. Information collected automatically

When using the App, these services may collect:

  • Device identifiers: Android Advertising ID and app-instance identifiers.
  • Usage data: mini-games opened, rounds played, scores, time spent, levels reached, games unlocked, and session information.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash or performance information.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from IP address (not GPS location).
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded ads watched.

6. Notifications

The App can show reminder notifications (for example when a new mini-game is unlocked). They are scheduled on your device and no data is sent to us for them. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings (Settings › Apps › Tap Toybox › Notifications).

7. Third-party services

These services process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the App. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database) – helps understand App usage, creates the anonymous player ID, stores leaderboard and friends data, and runs live matches with friends. Firebase privacy information
  • Tenjin – measures advertising campaign performance. Tenjin Privacy Policy

8. How we use information

  • To run the leaderboards and show your profile to other players.
  • To run the Friends feature: friend requests, online status, invites, and live matches.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases.
  • To show ads, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • To understand player preferences, fix problems, and improve the App.
  • To measure advertising campaign performance.

Personal information is not sold and is used only as described in this policy.

9. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland receive a consent message through Google before personalized ads display. Non-personalized ads may still appear if consent is declined. Privacy options in the App's settings allow users to change choices where available.

10. Your choices

  • Nickname and avatar: change them at any time in the Profile screen.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Friends: remove any friend in the Friends screen.
  • Leaderboard and friends data: ask us to delete it (see Data Deletion). Uninstalling the App does not remove your leaderboard entry by itself.
  • Your game data: deleting the App or clearing its storage removes progress from your device.

11. Data retention

Leaderboard and friends data are kept while these features are operated, or until you ask us to delete them. Pending invites expire after a short time. Purchase records are kept by Google Play according to Google's policies. Usage data in Firebase Analytics is retained for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted. AdMob and Tenjin retain data according to their own policies.

12. Your rights

Depending on location (for example under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or object to processing. To make a request about your leaderboard profile or friends data, email us your nickname and the player ID shown at the bottom of the Profile screen.

13. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Personal information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data sent by the App is transmitted over encrypted connections using reputable providers. Leaderboard and friends data are protected by access rules so that each player can change only their own profile. While no transmission method is completely secure, only necessary data is collected.

15. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted with a new effective date.
