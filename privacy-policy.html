Privacy Policy – Tap Toybox

Developer: Hamoroj · App: Tap Toybox (com.mohamad.minigames) · Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when playing Tap Toybox ("the App"), how it is used, and available choices. The App is developed by Hamoroj ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • The App has no email or password accounts and never asks for your email, phone number, or photos.
  • Your game progress (level, coins, scores, settings) is saved on your device.
  • For the leaderboards, the App creates an anonymous player ID and stores your nickname, avatar, XP, and best scores online. Other players can see your nickname, avatar, and scores.
  • You can add friends with a friend code and play live matches against them. For this, the App stores your friends list, friend requests, match invitations, head-to-head results, and whether you are online. There is no chat, no voice, and no matchmaking with strangers.
  • Third-party services from Google (AdMob and Firebase) and Tenjin (attribution partner) are used to show ads, run the leaderboards and online play, understand App usage, and measure advertising.
  • The App is not directed to children under 13.

2. Leaderboards and player profile

To show global and weekly leaderboards, the App stores the following on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to an anonymous player ID created by Firebase Authentication:

  • Nickname: a random name (for example "Player4821") that you can change in the Profile screen. Please do not use your real name.
  • Avatar: the character and color you pick in the App.
  • Game results: total XP, XP earned this week, and your best score in each mini-game.
  • Anonymous player ID and the time of the last update.

Your nickname, avatar, and scores are visible to other players in the leaderboards. Your player ID is shown at the bottom of the Profile screen so you can use it for data requests.

3. Friends and online play

You can add other players as friends and challenge them to live matches. You can only play against players who are on your friends list. To make this work, the App stores the following, linked to your anonymous player ID:

  • Friend code: a short random code (for example "K4X-9PQ") that belongs to your player ID. Anyone you give your code to can look up your nickname, avatar, and level and send you a friend request. The App can open WhatsApp so you can share your code; it only sends the message you choose to send.
  • Friend requests and friends list: who sent a request to whom (with the sender's nickname, avatar, and level), and the list of players you accepted as friends. Your friends can see your nickname, avatar, and level.
  • Online status: while the App is open, it regularly records that you are online and whether you are on the home screen, playing a game, or in a match, together with the time. Your friends can see this status.
  • Match invitations: when you challenge a friend, an invitation with your nickname, avatar, level, and the chosen mini-game is sent to that friend. Invitations expire after about 30 seconds.
  • Match data: during a match, the App exchanges game information with your friend's device: your score, positions and moves in the game, whether you are ready for another round, and a connection-speed measurement (ping). Your friend sees this live on their screen.
  • Head-to-head results: how many matches you and each friend have won against each other (for example "3 – 1"). Both of you can see this result.

Coins and XP you earn from matches are saved on your device, and your total XP is shown in the leaderboards as described above. Friends and match data are stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Realtime Database). Realtime match data is stored in the European Union (Belgium); other Firebase data may be stored in the United States.

4. Information collected automatically

When using the App, these services may collect:

  • Device identifiers: Android Advertising ID and app-instance identifiers.
  • Usage data: mini-games opened, rounds played, scores, time spent, levels reached, games unlocked, and session information.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash or performance information.
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from IP address (not GPS location).
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded ads watched.

5. Notifications

The App can show reminder notifications (for example when a new mini-game is unlocked). They are scheduled on your device and no data is sent to us for them. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings (Settings › Apps › Tap Toybox › Notifications).

6. Third-party services

These services process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the App. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database) – helps understand App usage, creates the anonymous player ID, stores leaderboard and friends data, and runs live matches. Firebase privacy information
  • Tenjin – measures advertising campaign performance. Tenjin Privacy Policy

7. How we use information

  • To run the leaderboards and show your profile to other players.
  • To let you add friends, see which friends are online, send and receive match invitations, play live matches, and keep your head-to-head results.
  • To show ads, including personalized ads where permitted.
  • To understand player preferences, fix problems, and improve the App.
  • To measure advertising campaign performance.

Personal information is not sold and is used only as described in this policy.

8. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland receive a consent message through Google before personalized ads display. Non-personalized ads may still appear if consent is declined. Privacy options in the App's settings allow users to change choices where available.

9. Your choices

  • Nickname and avatar: change them at any time in the Profile screen.
  • Friends: you decide who gets your friend code and which requests you accept. You can decline any request, and remove a friend at any time with the × button in the Friends screen; this removes the friendship for both of you. You do not have to use friends or online play at all.
  • Invitations: you can decline any match invitation or leave a match at any time.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Leaderboard profile and friends data: ask us to delete them (see Data Deletion). Uninstalling the App does not remove them by itself.
  • Your game data: deleting the App or clearing its storage removes progress from your device.

10. Data retention

Leaderboard data is kept while the leaderboard feature is operated, or until you ask us to delete it. Your friend code, friends list, and head-to-head results are kept until you remove the friend or ask us to delete your data. Friend requests are kept until they are accepted or declined. Your online status is overwritten every time it updates. Match invitations and match data are only needed during the match; old invitations and match data are deleted regularly. Usage data in Firebase Analytics is retained for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted. AdMob and Tenjin retain data according to their own policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on location (for example under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or object to processing. To make a request about your leaderboard profile or friends data, email us your nickname and the player ID shown at the bottom of the Profile screen.

12. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Personal information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data sent by the App is transmitted over encrypted connections using reputable providers. Online data is protected by access rules: each player can change only their own profile and status, friend lists can only be read by their owner, players can only become friends after a request is accepted, and each player can only add their own wins to a head-to-head result. Signed-in players who know your player ID may be able to see your online status. While no transmission method is completely secure, only necessary data is collected.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be posted with a new effective date.
