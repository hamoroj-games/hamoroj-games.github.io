Privacy Policy – Tap Toybox

Developer: Hamoroj · App: Tap Toybox (com.mohamad.minigames) · Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Tap Toybox ("the App"), how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is developed by Hamoroj ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, or photos.
  • Your game progress (level, coins, scores, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • We use third-party services from Google (AdMob and Firebase Analytics) and an attribution partner (Tenjin) to show ads, understand how the App is used, and measure our advertising.
  • The App is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the App, the following information may be collected automatically by the services listed in section 3:

  • Device identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and app-instance identifiers.
  • Usage data: which mini-games you open, rounds played, scores, time spent, levels reached, games unlocked, and session information.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash or performance information.
  • Approximate location: country or region, derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded ads watched.

3. Third-party services

These services process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the App. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps
  • Google Firebase Analytics – helps us understand how the App is used so we can improve it. Firebase privacy information
  • Tenjin – measures which advertising campaigns lead to installs of the App. Tenjin Privacy Policy

4. How we use information

  • To show ads, including personalized ads where you have allowed them.
  • To understand which games players enjoy, fix problems, and improve the App.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising campaigns.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose not described in this policy.

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. Where available, you can change your choice later from the privacy options in the App's settings.

6. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the App stops all further collection.
  • Your game data: deleting the App or clearing its storage removes your progress from your device.

7. Data retention

Usage data in Firebase Analytics is kept for a limited period (up to 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Data processed by AdMob and Tenjin is kept according to their own policies linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot link data to your name. To make a request, contact us at the email below and we will help as far as we are able.

9. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Security

All data sent by the App to the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use reputable providers and collect only what the App needs.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
